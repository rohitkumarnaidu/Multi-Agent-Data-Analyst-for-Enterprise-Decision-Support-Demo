# Demo Questions

The following 5 questions are designed to test the capabilities of the Multi-Agent Data Analyst system.

1. **SQL Lookups**: "What are the top 3 product categories by total sales revenue in our historical data?"
2. **Predictive Modeling**: "Predict the late delivery risk for a new order with these specs: Weight 1200g, Volume 2500cm3, Freight 22.5, Price 89.9, Freight ratio 0.25, Approval hrs 1.5, ordered on Wednesday (2) in November (11), seller late rate 0.12, Customer state MG, Seller state SP."
3. **Hybrid Execution (SQL + Visualization)**: "What is the historical average freight value for the top 5 customer states? Please generate a bar chart for this data."
4. **Analytical Summarization**: "How does the late delivery rate compare between orders placed in Q1 versus Q4? Use the database to find out."
5. **Edge Case (No Data)**: "Can you provide the late delivery rate for orders placed in the year 2050?"
